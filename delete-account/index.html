Delete Your Qodex Account and Data

At Qodex, you have full control over your personal data. You can delete your account and all associated data inside the app, or by sending us a request.

Option 1: In-app deletion (instant, recommended)

  1. Open the Qodex app on your device.
  2. Tap the Settings icon (⚙️) on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the About section.
  4. Tap “Delete Account & Data” (🗑️).
  5. Tap “Delete” in the confirmation dialog.

Result: your account is immediately deleted from Firebase Authentication, and all local history, cached images, and preferences are permanently wiped from your device.

Option 2: Email request

If you have uninstalled Qodex or cannot access your device, send an email:

  • To: support@qodexscanner.com
  • Subject: Account & Data Deletion Request - Qodex
  • Include: the email address you registered with (Google Sign-In, Facebook Sign-In, or email login) and this request: “Please permanently delete my Qodex user account and all associated personal data.”

Processing time: after verifying ownership, we permanently delete your user record from Firebase Authentication and cloud databases within 7 business days, then send you a confirmation email.
