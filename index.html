Frequently asked questions

What is a custom ERP system, and do I need one?

A custom ERP is business software built around your own processes: sales, inventory, accounts, HR, production and reports in one system. If spreadsheets or off-the-shelf tools no longer fit how you work, a custom ERP removes double entry and gives you live data.

Which apps and websites does Lumix Tech build?

We build Android and iOS apps, business websites, web apps, e-commerce stores, admin dashboards and AI automation tools.

How long does a project take and what does it cost?

It depends on scope. Send us your requirements and you get a plan, timeline and price within two working days.

What is Qodex?

Qodex is our own QR and barcode scanner app for Android, with an AI universal decoder, 300+ QR frames, batch scanning and price lookup.

Do you support the project after launch?

Yes. We monitor, update and support every product after release.
